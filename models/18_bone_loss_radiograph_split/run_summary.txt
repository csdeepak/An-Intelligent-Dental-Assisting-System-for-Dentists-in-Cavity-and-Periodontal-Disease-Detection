THE LABEL IS NOT PERIODONTAL BONE LOSS. Positive = the tooth crop overlaps a Tufts expert
ABNORMALITY mask by more than 1% (apical lesions, cysts, sclerosis). This is an abnormality
flag. See PAPER_MATERIAL.md Sec 8.28.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
WHY THIS EXISTS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The shipped model's 0.9233 macro-F1 was validation on its own training radiographs. Scored on
821 Tufts radiographs it never trained on it finds 7.6% of its own label (RESULTS_LOG
2026-10-13). This run asks whether the same architecture and recipe learn the label when the
split is by radiograph and all the training-split radiographs are used.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
RECIPE -- the original's (notebooks/bone_loss/03), three stated differences
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
ResNet18, ImageNet weights, all layers trained. Focal loss, gamma 2.0, alpha [0.6428, 2.2502].
Adam 1e-4, weight decay 1e-4, batch 32. ReduceLROnPlateau on validation macro-F1 (0.5, 5).
At most 50 epochs, stop after 10 without improvement, keep the best validation macro-F1.
Flip, rotation within 10 degrees, brightness x[0.8, 1.2], contrast x[0.9, 1.1].
Differences: (1) split by radiograph; (2) 678 training radiographs, not 111; (3) an epoch is
every diseased crop once and 3.5 normals for each, redrawn every epoch (1,585 + 5,548), where
the original kept one fixed set of 2,500 normals.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
WHAT THIS IS AND IS NOT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
It is evidence that, with a radiograph-level split and six times the radiographs, this recipe
learns something the shipped model did not: it beats the shipped model and a tooth-position
prior on radiographs none of them trained on, on all three seeds.
It is weak in absolute terms. Seed 42 finds about one labelled tooth in four at about one false
flag per radiograph. Which seed is trained moves sensitivity between 11% and 27%.
It has no external figure and cannot get one from Ningxia, which is locked. The 2.17% on
Ningxia and the 0.75% on DENTEX belong to the shipped model.
It does not make Stage 4 periodontal.
