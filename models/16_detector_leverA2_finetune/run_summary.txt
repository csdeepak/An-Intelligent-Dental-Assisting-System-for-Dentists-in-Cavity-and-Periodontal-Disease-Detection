--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
WHAT A2 IS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Phase 0 (RESULTS_LOG 2026-09-04) diagnosed Lever A's Guard-2 failure as a
CONFIDENCE-CALIBRATION COLLAPSE on the minority domain -- the multi-corpus backbone
finds the Tufts teeth (recall 0.80 at conf 0.10) but the score stops separating
real teeth from spurious boxes. A2 tests the fix that diagnosis implies:

  load models/15_detector_multicorpus_leverA/arm3c_best.pt (the frozen 3-corpus
  Lever A checkpoint), FREEZE the backbone (freeze=11 -> layers 0-10, 50.7% of
  params), and fine-tune the neck (11-22) + Detect head (23) on Tufts-only
  (train_tufts.txt, 700 images) for 40 epochs / patience 12 / warmup 1, canonical
  LR 1e-3. Hypothesis: re-specialise the Tufts head calibration while the frozen
  backbone keeps the +34 pp DENTEX / +9 pp external gains.

Dataset: a Tufts-only subset of data/detector_multicorpus_v2/ (280 MB tar,
sha256 450212868b..., box counts val 3794 / test 3924 reproduced -- on-box GATE: GO).
A2 touches Tufts only, so DENTEX/Kaggle/UFBA images were left out of the upload.

Training: EarlyStopping at epoch 18, best.pt = epoch 6. Tufts-VAL mAP@0.5 rose
0.542 (epoch 0 = arm3c) -> ~0.62 (epoch 5) then plateaued.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
VERDICT (pre-registered rule, bar NOT moved)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
PRIMARY passes (+29 pp), GUARD 1 passes (+8.6 pp), GUARD 2 FAILS (0.593 vs 0.684).
=> 2 of 3.  ABLATION / not adopt-worthy.  Pipeline default detector unchanged.

This is pre-registration §5 row 3: "GUARD 2 fails (Tufts does not recover) -> head
fine-tuning is insufficient; the box-mAP ceiling is the box-vs-mask supervision gap
(PAPER_MATERIAL §8.9), not the training mix." The Phase-0 calibration hypothesis
predicted a frozen-backbone head fine-tune would recover Tufts. It recovered Tufts
VALIDATION mAP but NOT Tufts TEST mAP, and got nowhere near the from-scratch
Tufts detector (0.593 vs 0.724). The ceiling is deeper than the detection head:
the frozen 3-corpus backbone features cannot be recombined into a Control-B-quality
Tufts detector by the neck + head alone.

The only untried detector lever is multi-corpus SEGMENTATION (pre-reg Phase 2b) --
scoped as post-submission work, NOT a next step under the ISBI timeline.
