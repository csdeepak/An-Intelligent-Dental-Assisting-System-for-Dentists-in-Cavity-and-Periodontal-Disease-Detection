ARCHITECTURE — and the one part that is not obvious
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  ResNet18 (ImageNet-pretrained) over a 1.5x-expanded 224x224 tooth crop,
  concatenated with 4 positional features [cx, cy, bw, bh] (normalised by the FULL image,
  not the crop) through a 4->64->64 MLP, then two linear heads:
      quadrant  (4-way)   position (8-way)      FDI = quadrant*10 + position

  WHY POSITIONAL FEATURES ARE LOAD-BEARING, NOT A CONVENIENCE:
  teeth 16 and 26 are near mirror images, as are 36/46, 11/21 and every other left/right
  pair. Laterality is not recoverable from crop pixels at all — a crop-only classifier
  would be guessing on half of every label. Normalised x resolves it. Geometry alone already
  gives 93% quadrant accuracy; what the IMAGE contributes is the position digit, which is
  exactly where geometry has a hard ceiling. The two signals are complementary and the
  architecture reflects that.

  TWO HEADS RATHER THAN A 32-WAY SOFTMAX: so a wrong FDI is diagnostic — it says whether the
  error was the wrong side of the mouth or the wrong tooth along the arch.

  PRIOR ART: DPML (Cao et al., Expert Systems With Applications 254:124446, 2024) section 3.4
  argues the same design independently. Its 8-class formulation, justified by bilateral
  symmetry, sidesteps the left/right problem that FDI's 32 classes cannot.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
TRAINING
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Data      DENTEX training_data/quadrant_enumeration — 634 images, 18,095 teeth, all 32 FDI
            classes (341-630 examples each). FDI = (category_id_1+1)*10 + (category_id_2+1),
            verified spatially before use, not assumed.
  Split     BY IMAGE, 85/15: 538 train images (15,366 teeth -> 30,732 crops after one
            jittered copy each) / 96 val images (2,729 teeth). Zero images and zero
            annotation ids span the split; all jittered copies are train-only.
  Optimiser AdamW, lr 1e-3, cosine annealing over 15 epochs, batch 64, seed 42
  Augment   RandomRotation(7) + ColorJitter(0.2, 0.2). NO HORIZONTAL FLIP — it mirrors the
            arch and would invert the quadrant label. Training crops also carry the +/-15%
            box jitter the detector produces at inference.
  Hardware  Apple M4 Pro, MPS, 48 minutes (190.7 s/epoch)
  Selection Best validation FDI-exact; epoch 13.

RESULTS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
HELD-OUT — DENTEX disease/, 164 leak-free images, 936 GT teeth, DETECTOR boxes.
This is the reportable number. Both rows use the identical detector, settings and matching.

                             positional (replaced)    THIS MODEL
  detection recall                  67.0%               67.0%
  quadrant correct (of detected)    92.3%               94.7%
  FDI exact (of detected)           34.1%               69.4%
  FDI exact, END-TO-END             22.9%               46.5%
  duplicate FDI codes                   0                   0

  A 2.0x improvement on both FDI measures.

  THE CEILING ARGUMENT — the substantive claim:
  positional assignment reaches only 66.3% even on FULLY-DETECTED (8-detection) quadrants,
  because eight detected teeth are not positions 1-8, they are eight teeth that survived
  extraction, and rank order cannot distinguish those cases. On that SAME population this
  model reaches 80.2% — clearing the ceiling by +13.9 points (n=101). It wins in every
  completeness bucket. Not a better sorter: a method not subject to the constraint.

  [CORRECTED 24 Aug 2026] This block previously read "67.0% ceiling ... +2.4 points".
  67.0% is the detector's RECALL, not the ceiling, and the +2.4 compared the ceiling
  (complete quadrants only) with this model's accuracy over ALL detected teeth. Recompute:
  scripts/eval/analyze_stage2_ceiling.py -> outputs/stage2_clean/stage2_ceiling_analysis.json
  See RESULTS_LOG 2026-08-24a.

  END-TO-END IS NOW CAPPED BY STAGE 1. At 67.0% detector recall, 67.0% is the maximum
  achievable end-to-end; this model captures 69.4% of it. Detector recall is the binding
  constraint from here, not numbering.

  ONE-TO-ONE ASSIGNMENT (Hungarian over the 32 codes) is part of the method, not an option.
  Measured on the clean 164-image set (24 Aug 2026):
                              of-detected   end-to-end   quadrant   duplicate codes
    Hungarian one-to-one         69.4%        46.5%        94.7%           0
    plain argmax                 65.1%        43.6%        96.8%         679
  The constraint removes ALL 679 impossible codes and gains +4.3 / +2.9 points, costing 2.1
  points of raw quadrant accuracy - the global constraint sometimes moves a tooth across a
  quadrant boundary. Worth taking: a duplicate code is not a slightly-wrong answer, it is an
  impossible one a downstream report cannot render.
  (Supersedes the contaminated-set figures 72.7/52.2 vs 70.6/50.7/1,013. RESULTS_LOG 24a/24b.)

VALIDATION (GT boxes, enumeration val split) — A DIAGNOSTIC, NOT A RESULT:
  quadrant 99.4%, position 92.1%, FDI exact 91.6% (macro-F1 0.916).
  These are SELECTION-BIASED: the checkpoint was chosen on this same split. They also use
  ground-truth boxes rather than detector boxes. Do not quote them as performance.

  Per-position accuracy is notably FLAT — 0.886 to 0.963 across all eight positions —
  unlike the detector, whose recall collapses from 98.3% (position 1) to 61.0% (position 8).
  The head does not inherit Stage 1's posterior weakness.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
EVALUATION DISCIPLINE — read before reproducing or citing
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  86 of the 250 DENTEX disease/ images are EXCLUDED as training duplicates: 85 are
  byte-identical (MD5) to quadrant_enumeration images used to train this model, and 1
  (test_181) to a detector training image. 164 remain.

  A first evaluation reported 52.2% end-to-end before this was found — an inflation of
  5.7 points. The cause is worth remembering: quadrant_enumeration and the disease SUBSET
  (705 images) genuinely are disjoint patients, and that was verified — but disease/ is a
  THIRD folder sharing a third of its images with the enumeration set. A verified fact about
  one pair was generalised to a pair that had never been measured.

  FOR ANY FUTURE EVALUATION SET, INCLUDING NINGXIA: measure overlap against EACH training
  corpus separately. Do not infer disjointness from a neighbouring pair.

  Inference preprocessing was verified to reproduce training preprocessing exactly:
  200/200 predictions agree on the same teeth through both code paths.
