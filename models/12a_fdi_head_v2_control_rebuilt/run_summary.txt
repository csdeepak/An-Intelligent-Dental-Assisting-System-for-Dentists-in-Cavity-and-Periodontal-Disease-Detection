-----------------------------------------------------------------
 WHY IT WAS TRAINED
-----------------------------------------------------------------
 Two independent things changed on 28 Aug before v3 could be trained:

 1. The DENTEX and Tufts enumeration crops were REBUILT locally — they were
    not on this machine at all, only the trained v2 head was.
 2. Rebuilding exposed a geometry defect: every Tufts radiograph here is
    840x1615, rotated 90 degrees from the 1615x840 frame the crop builder
    expects. The first rebuild silently produced 28,171/2,249 train/val with
    10,087 degenerate crops (23%) instead of the documented 35,248/3,721.
    See RESULTS_LOG.md 2026-08-28c.

 Comparing v3 against the PUBLISHED v2 numbers would therefore have folded
 the crop rebuild, the geometry fix, a torch version change (2.11 vs the
 original) and a different GPU into what is supposed to be a single-variable
 test of "does a third corpus help". So v2 was retrained from scratch on the
 rebuilt crops, and v3 is compared against this instead.

 METRICS — reproduces v2.0 to within ~1 pp
-----------------------------------------------------------------
                        published v2.0     this control     delta
   val quadrant             0.9811            0.983         +0.2 pp
   val position             0.8253            0.833         +0.8 pp
   val FDI exact            0.8101            0.821         +1.1 pp

 Consistent with run-to-run variance across a different torch and GPU, not a
 systematic difference. This is what clears the geometry fix: a head trained on
 misaligned crops does not land on 98.3% quadrant accuracy — it collapses.

   Kaggle test (1,994)   quadrant 94.5%  position 82.0%  FDI exact 77.7%
     ^ Kaggle is entirely UNSEEN by this head — it is the zero-shot cross-corpus
       reference against which v3's 93.2% is measured.
