SEVERITY DEFINITION — a proxy, and it must be described as one
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
CORRECTION, 13 Oct 2026 -- the paragraph below describes what the notebook was
meant to do, not what the run that produced these weights did. That run found
no overlap column in its metadata (columns: filename, label, stem, augmented),
printed "overlap column not found; building proxy overlap from image
contrast", and labelled every crop from the standard deviation of its grey
levels, rescaled to 0.01-0.30 and cut at the same 0.05 / 0.15. The three
classes are therefore LOW / MIDDLE / HIGH CROP CONTRAST. The saved manifests
(data/tufts/severity_dataset/metadata/) carry that rescaled column. Two of the
32 training radiographs (149, 192) and one of the 9 validation radiographs
(249) are in the Tufts test split. Evidence: the saved output of cell 5 of
notebooks/bone_loss/04_bone_loss_severity.ipynb. RESULTS_LOG 2026-10-13d.

Labels come from the overlap ratio between the expert per-image bone-loss mask
and the per-tooth crop, NOT from a clinical severity grading:
    Mild      overlap <= 0.05
    Moderate  overlap 0.05 - 0.15
    Severe    overlap  > 0.15
The 0.05 / 0.15 cut points are undocumented as to provenance -- whether tuned or
chosen by eye is not recorded, and PAPER_MATERIAL.md 9 lists writing that up as
an open item. Treat the class as "extent of annotated involvement", not as a
clinical stage.

CLASS ORDER
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
src/classifiers.py declares BONE_LOSS_SEVERITY_CLASSES = ["Mild","Moderate",
"Severe"]. The checkpoint stores no class_to_idx. Two things agree with that order:
  1. severity_summary.txt reports its per-class scores in exactly this order.
  2. It is the ImageFolder alphabetical order of {Mild, Moderate, Severe}, which
     is how the training notebook built its loaders.
Weaker than Stage 4a's confirmation -- there is no arithmetic cross-check here,
because the per-class alphas were not recorded numerically. Flagged, not hidden.

OPEN ITEM — T0.5, still undecided
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The Mild class has n=6. PUBLICATION_TODO.md T0.5 asks whether to merge it, report
3-class with explicit support, or drop severity reporting; the decision has not
been made and no bootstrap CIs exist anywhere. This is a reporting decision, not a
retrain. Tagging these weights does not close T0.5.

INHERITS Stage 4a's DOMAIN-SHIFT FAILURE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4b only sees teeth 4a flagged. Since 4a fires on 0.7% of DENTEX teeth and has
0.8-5.4% affected-tooth sensitivity on Ningxia, out of domain this stage is
effectively never exercised. Its in-domain 0.7208 says nothing about external
behaviour.
