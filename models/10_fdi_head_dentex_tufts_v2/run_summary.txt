-----------------------------------------------------------------
 WHY v2 EXISTS — robustness, NOT in-domain accuracy
-----------------------------------------------------------------
v2 does not beat v1 in-domain and was never expected to. It was adopted
because it holds up on a second machine population at no in-domain cost.
Do not quote it as an accuracy improvement.

                        DENTEX 164 (e2e)   Tufts test (3,786 crops)
  v1.0  DENTEX only          46.47%              50.0%
  v2.0  DENTEX + Tufts       46.58%              71.5%

  in-domain  : +0.11pp — inside the ~1pt run-to-run noise floor measured in
               RESULTS_LOG 2026-08-25i (two IDENTICAL configs differed by 9
               teeth of 936). NOT an improvement.
  out-domain : +21.5pp — the reason for the swap. RESULTS_LOG 2026-08-25j.

A Tufts-ONLY head (arm C) was also trained and rejected: it loses 9.1 points
on DENTEX (37.39%) while beating v2 on Tufts by an amount that is not
significant (McNemar p=0.732). v2 strictly dominates it.
