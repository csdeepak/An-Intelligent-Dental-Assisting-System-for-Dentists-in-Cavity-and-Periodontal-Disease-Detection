--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
WHY THIS EXISTS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
B-single (models/14, trained on CariesXrays only) wins CariesXrays decisively but
collapses out-of-domain on DENTEX (8.6% tooth-level lesion agreement vs comparators'
44-49%, RESULTS_LOG 2026-09-04f). B-pooled adds DENTEX-domain caries-box training data
(coarser, whole-tooth boxes rather than lesion boxes -- a domain adapter, not a second
lesion-box source) to test whether the transfer gap closes without giving up the
CariesXrays win.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
CONFIG NOTES / DEVIATIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- batch: 16, not the canonical 32 -- B-single's own training hit CUDA OOM at 32 on an
  A100-40GB (RESULTS_LOG 2026-09-02b); 16 avoids repeating that AND keeps this arm's
  realised recipe identical to B-single's realised (not nominal) one.
- mosaic=0.0, flipud=0.0 by house rule (medical-imaging spatial-context / anatomy).
- epochs 120 / patience 25, matching B-single -- cost cap, pre-registered.
- Early-stopped at epoch 54 -- shorter than B-single's 86, consistent with the lower
  total spend relative to the pre-registered ceiling.
- `project=` behaviour: ultralytics saved this run under
  `runs/detect/runs_lever_b/lever_b_pooled_yolo11l-2/` on the training box, NOT under
  the plain `runs_lever_b/lever_b_pooled_yolo11l/` the trainer script's own arguments
  implied (ultralytics silently prepends its internal `runs_dir` setting, and a stale
  dir from a crashed first launch attempt added the `-2` suffix). Recorded so the next
  script that shells out to `model.train(project=..., ...)` checks the REAL output
  path rather than trusting the argument it passed.

Full Sec 6 table: DENTEX fail (tie), CariesXrays PASS, Ningxia PASS. 2 of 3 >= 2 of 3,

and is reported as a tie everywhere -- this is not "solved on every cohort".

Ningxia is the only one of the three cohorts this detector never trained on, so it is
the only external evidence in the table. That is stated in the paper rather than
glossed: pooling bought DENTEX transfer at the cost of DENTEX's status as held-out data.

PARAMETER COUNT, to prevent a false drift report: 25.28 M above is the FUSED count that

of the same network.
