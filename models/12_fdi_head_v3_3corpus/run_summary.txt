-----------------------------------------------------------------
 HOW TO READ THIS
-----------------------------------------------------------------
 * The Kaggle +15.45 pp is NOT a generalisation result. v3 trained on that
   corpus; the control had never seen it. It says "training on a corpus helps
   on that corpus". It is reported because omitting the largest number would
   look like concealment, not because it supports a claim.
 * The real result is DENTEX +1.14 pp, which survives its interval: a third
   corpus improved accuracy on a corpus it added no data to.
 * Tufts is FLAT. +0.74 pp with a CI crossing zero, and near-balanced churn
   (294 crops v3 fixed, 266 it broke). Report as no change, not as a gain.
 * Nothing regressed on any corpus or any metric.
 * What this buys the paper is a THREE-CORPUS claim — a statement about
   breadth, not a headline metric moving.

 A +1.14 pp in-domain gain does not justify that before the 14 Oct freeze.
