THE LABEL IS NOT A CLINICAL SEVERITY AND NOT PERIODONTAL. It is an EXTENT BAND OF AN
EXPERT-MARKED ABNORMALITY: the share of the 1.5x W / 2.0x H tooth crop that overlaps a Tufts
expert abnormality mask (mostly apical lesions, sclerosis, cysts), cut at 0.05 and 0.15 into
bands 0 / 1 / 2, on teeth whose overlap exceeds 0.01 (the Stage-4a positives). The class names
"Mild / Moderate / Severe" of src/classifiers.py are index names only.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
WHY THIS EXISTS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The shipped Stage-4b head (models/04) was trained on bands of crop CONTRAST, a stand-in its
notebook built when the overlap column was missing (RESULTS_LOG 2026-10-13d). This trains the
same architecture on the quantity it was named for, with a radiograph-level split. The decision
owner chose retraining over removal on 14 Oct (D10).

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
RECIPE -- protocol Sec 2, as committed
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
ResNet18, ImageNet weights, 3 outputs, all layers trained. Cross-entropy weighted
n / (3 n_c) over the training bands. Adam 1e-4, weight decay 1e-4, batch 32.
ReduceLROnPlateau on validation macro-F1 (0.5, patience 5). At most 50 epochs, stop after 10
without improvement, keep the best validation macro-F1. Flip, rotation within 10 degrees,
brightness x[0.8, 1.2], contrast x[0.9, 1.1].

Reading the histories. Each epoch's `lr` is logged after that epoch's scheduler step, so it is
the rate the NEXT epoch uses (seed 42 trained epoch 9 at 1e-4; its row shows 5e-5).
`best_epoch` is taken from the 4-dp rounded values; selection used the unrounded ones. No tie
arises in these three runs.

Run notes. The three runs were made sequentially in one background chain that stopped on the
first failure; none failed, none was repeated. Each log has, after its first line, two macOS
`objc` warnings about AVF classes present in both PyAV and OpenCV's bundled libavdevice -- an
environment duplicate unrelated to training; nothing in the runs touches video.
Validation (and checkpoint selection) ran on MPS; the test pass scores on CPU, so a prediction
on a near-tie can differ from what MPS would give.
