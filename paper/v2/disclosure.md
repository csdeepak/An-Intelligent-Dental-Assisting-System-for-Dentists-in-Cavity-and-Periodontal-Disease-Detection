## What the AI did and did not do (record for the authors)

- **Did:** wrote the text from the confirmed claims; built the evidence and reference records; drew
  Figure 5 with `scripts/figures/make_v2_operating_curve.py`; reused the authors' existing figures;
  ran the skill's deterministic checks; dispatched the isolated integrity and panel reviews.
- **Did not:** run any experiment, train or evaluate any model, change any saved result, or choose the
  venue, the scope, or the claims. The authors chose those.
- **Isolation:** the integrity verifier and the panel ran as fresh Claude Code subagents. They were
  separate instances of the same model family, not independent human reviewers.
