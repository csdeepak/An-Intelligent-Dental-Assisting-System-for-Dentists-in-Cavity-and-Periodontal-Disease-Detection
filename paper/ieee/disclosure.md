# AI-use disclosure (draft for the authors -- edit to the venue's policy)

During preparation of this manuscript draft the authors used the Research Communication Engine (RCE v0.4, a local
skill with deterministic checking tools) with the Claude Opus 5.5 model. In the first version (30 Sep 2026) it built
an evidence map from the project's saved evaluation artifacts, drafted sections from that map, and ran two blind
review rounds with an isolated reviewer instance of the same model. In the rebuilt version (28 Oct 2026) it rewrote
the draft on the numbers the authors' maintained manuscript verifies, and wrote the script that renders Figures 1-3
and 5 by running the authors' own trained pipeline on public DENTEX radiographs and reading the models' own training
logs; a check confirmed that every number in the draft traces to that manuscript or to a saved file. No image was
AI-generated: every radiograph is a DENTEX image and every mark on it is the output of the authors' models or the
dataset's expert annotation. Literature metadata was verified against Crossref and arXiv records; cited works were
read at abstract level only. All results come from the authors' own experiments; the authors are responsible for
verifying the content before any submission.
