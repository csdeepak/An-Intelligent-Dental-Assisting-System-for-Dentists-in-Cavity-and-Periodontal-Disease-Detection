WHAT WAS TESTED, AND UNDER WHAT RULE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
outputs/caries_arms/PRE_REGISTRATION.md was written and committed BEFORE any arm
was trained. It fixed, in advance:

  primary metric   average precision on the 863-crop held-out test split
  comparator       a same-day control (v3 config, frozen crops_v3_phase1, today)
  success          paired AP delta vs control with a 95% CI EXCLUDING zero
  interval         paired cluster bootstrap over the 34 source images,
                   5,000 resamples, seed 42

AP rather than precision/recall because the v4 experiment had just shown why:
there, precision rose 3.6 pp and recall fell 8.7 pp while AP did not move at all.
The operating point had slid along an unchanged curve. AP is the only one of the
four that answers "did the curve lift".

  arm A  CLAHE      clip 2.0, 8x8 tiles, applied to the source radiograph
  arm B  exp125     crop box grown 1.25x about its centre before the 224 resize
  arm C  balance    WeightedRandomSampler equalising each (tooth type x label) cell

THE CONTROL IS FAITHFUL
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Control best val macro-F1 = 0.7350, which is EXACTLY v3's published val macro-F1
(model_metrics.md, arm B). Environment and torch-version drift are therefore not
confounding the arm deltas.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
PRECONDITIONS — verified before training, all passed
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Arms A and B rebuild the pixels, so the comparison is only paired if the crop SETS
are identical. Checked for both rebuilt corpora:
  * 6,616 crops; test split 863 with 127 positive
  * identical (crop, split, label, fdi) composition to crops_v3_phase1
  * identical set of source_image_md5
  * pixels genuinely differ: CLAHE mean abs diff 16.96 (contrast std 44.3 -> 62.0);
    exp125 mean abs diff 32.22
This is what makes the comparison item-for-item, and is exactly what the v4
experiment could NOT claim (its test split had 1,218 items, not 863).

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
VERDICT — the bar was not met and was NOT moved
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
NO ARM SUCCEEDS. All three AP intervals span zero.

**CLAHE misses by 0.0031** on the lower bound. It is recorded as a miss, in the
same terms as R1's mask bar (0.6383 against a pre-registered 0.65, missed by
0.0117, adopted anyway by a separate dated decision and never described as a pass).
The bar is not adjusted after seeing the number.

What IS real, and is reported as a secondary result:
  * CLAHE buys precision (+5.6 pp, CI excludes zero) with NO significant recall
    cost -- the only arm where that is true. Its FP rate falls in every stratum
    (premolar 0.268 -> 0.137, molar 0.482 -> 0.457).
  * exp125's precision gain is offset by a REAL recall loss. That is a shift along
    the curve, not a lift, which the flat AP confirms.

What did NOT happen, and was pre-registered as a possibility:
  * Arm C did NOT collapse. PRE_REGISTRATION.md warned that removing the position
    prior might LOWER AP, since the test population genuinely has 3.4x more caries
    in molars. It did not: AP +0.043, CI spanning zero. The prior is apparently not
    load-bearing for AP in the way the warning anticipated.

What CLAHE does NOT fix: the anterior blindness. Incisor recall stays 0.125 and
canine recall goes 0.200 -> 0.000. The position prior is still plainly present
(mean P on healthy teeth 0.037 incisor -> 0.443 molar). CLAHE reduces false
positives; it does not make the model see anterior lesions.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
THE HONEST LIMITATION OF THIS EXPERIMENT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
It is UNDERPOWERED. The test split has 863 crops but only **34 source images**, and
the intervals are clustered over images because teeth within a radiograph are not
independent. That is the correct choice and it is why the CIs are wide: a +0.049 AP
point estimate cannot be separated from zero at n=34. A larger held-out image set
would resolve CLAHE one way or the other; nothing else here will.
