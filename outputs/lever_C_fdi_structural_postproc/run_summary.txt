1. IMPLEMENTATION CHOICES SETTLED BEFORE THE FIRST FROZEN-SET RUN (§3 left these open)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
* DP recurrence: dp[k][d] = max over d' allowed by the skip rule of dp[k-1][d'] + log p_k(d),
  anchors pinned, O(N·8²) per quadrant, exact, no beam.
* Tie-breaking inside a run: the lowest digit wins (strict `>` on the score comparison), so the
  labelling is deterministic and independent of array/dict ordering.
* Spacing → skip: `gap_before[k] > 1.6 × median(gaps in that quadrant)` permits ANY jump, not a
  jump of a computed size — the spacing evidences *that* a tooth is missing, not how many.
* Feasibility ladder: skip-constrained DP → (if infeasible) the same DP with the skip rule
  relaxed to plain strict monotonicity → (if still infeasible, i.e. >8 boxes in one quadrant
  group) keep the Hungarian labels for that group and flag `structural_infeasible`.
* Quadrant grouping is the head's argmax, per §3 step 1 — NOT the Hungarian's jointly reassigned
  quadrant.
* Emitted confidence is recomputed as exp(log p(quadrant) + log p(position)), the same quantity
  `assign_fdi_head` reports, so Stage-5 filtering is unaffected.

5. WHY IT IS A NULL — the mechanism, measured
----------------------------------------------
On Tufts test the repair can only move a tooth that is (a) in a quadrant group of ≤8 detections
and (b) not itself an anchor. Measured over the 149 test images, 3,489 detections, 541 quadrant
groups (`structural_diagnostics` in stage2_summary_head__*.json):

  anchors, immovable by construction   2,534 / 3,489   72.6%
  in an over-full group (>8 boxes)       469 / 3,489   13.4%   (48 of 541 groups)
  no anchor in the group                     0 / 3,489    0.0%
  MOVABLE                                486 / 3,489   13.9%

So the method had 13.9% of the detections to work with, and inside that 13.9% it is further
constrained to labellings consistent with the anchors around them. A +0.29 pp effect on the
whole cohort is roughly a 2 pp effect on the slice it can actually touch.

Two independent things throttle it, both inherent to the frozen method rather than to this
implementation:

  * τ = 0.30 is permissive. It was frozen to the codebase's existing LOW_CONFIDENCE precisely so
    Lever C would introduce no tunable — but at 0.30 the head clears the anchor threshold on the
    large majority of teeth, so the longest-increasing-subsequence anchors most of the arch,
    INCLUDING teeth that are confidently wrong. A confidently wrong anchor is exactly an
    off-by-one error the constraint then has to route around instead of correcting.
  * Over-full groups. Detector boxes are noisy and the head's argmax quadrant concentrates, so a
    substantial share of teeth sit in groups holding more than 8 detections, where no strictly
    increasing labelling of 1..8 exists and the method is a no-op by construction.

The one place the constraint does what it was designed to do is the contiguous-arch stratum
(+2.36 pp on 296 detected teeth) — the subset where its assumption actually holds. That is a
real signal and it is also 9.5% of the data.
